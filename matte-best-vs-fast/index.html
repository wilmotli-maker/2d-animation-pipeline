best = BiRefNet-DIS (928 MB), the model the shipped with-alpha/ set was matted with. fast = isnet-general-use (179 MB). Both runs use identical despill and the same source clips — the promoted -vNNN take where one exists.

What the numbers do not cover: edge quality. There is no validated automated metric for it here, which is the entire reason for the videos below. The structural metrics (background, interior, antenna presence) say the two models are equivalent; whether fast’s wider, softer edge matters is a judgement call to make by eye.

Antennae — the thin-structure test

AI_ALT2’s antenna stalks narrow to 4 px. Across all 990 robot frames, neither model drops a single frame. The 38–39% dip on ai-alt2-talk-03 appears in both, which is what makes it genuine antenna motion rather than matte failure.
